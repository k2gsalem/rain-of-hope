Rain of Hope: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · App: Rain of Hope (io.github.k2gsalem.rainofhope)

Rain of Hope is a calm puzzle game. We built it to collect nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts and no sign-in. It does not have the internet permission, so it cannot send information anywhere.

What stays on your phone

  • Which levels you finished and how many flowers you earned
  • Your settings: sound, music, vibration, language, motion and accessibility choices, and your cloud shape
  • Whether you are under 13, from the one-time age question. Your birth year itself is not stored.

This data never leaves your phone through the app. If you turn on Android's own backup, Android may copy it to your Google account so you keep your progress on a new phone. You can erase it at any time in Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

Vibration: used for rain, lightning and the breathing guide. You can turn it off in Settings. Joy Dance reads the phone's motion sensor while that screen is open, only to detect dance moves. These readings are never saved or sent.

Children

Rain of Hope is suitable for everyone, including children. Because we collect no personal information, we do not collect personal information from children.

Sharing

If you tap Share after a level, Android opens its share menu with a short message. You choose where it goes; the app sends nothing by itself.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles data, such as purchases, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.

Contact

Questions or requests: email k7maligai@gmail.com, or open an issue at github.com/k2gsalem/rain-of-hope/issues.
