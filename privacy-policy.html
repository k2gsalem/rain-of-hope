Rain of Hope: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026 · App: Rain of Hope (io.github.k2gsalem.rainofhope)

Rain of Hope is a calm puzzle game. We, the developer, collect nothing about you.

What we collect

We collect nothing. The app has no ads, no analytics of our own, no crash reporting of our own, no accounts and no sign-in, and the game plays offline. The one exception is the optional Supporter Pack, described below, which uses Google Play.

Supporter Pack and Google Play

The Supporter Pack is an optional one-time purchase of cosmetic extras. It is offered only to players who answered the age question with 13 or older. Google's Play Billing library runs only for those players. It uses the internet and sends Google information about purchases and diagnostics, under Google's Privacy Policy. Google handles the payment. We never see your card, bank or UPI details. On your phone we keep only whether you own the pack, so the extras work offline.

What stays on your phone

  • Which levels you finished, how many flowers you earned, and your fewest moves on each level
  • The dates on which you solved the Daily Rain puzzle, and your streak, for the calendar
  • Levels you made in the Level Editor or imported from a code
  • Your settings: sound, music, vibration, language, motion and accessibility choices, and your chosen cloud
  • Whether you are under 13, from the one-time age question. Your birth year itself is not stored.

This data never leaves your phone through the app. If you turn on Android's own backup, Android may copy it to your Google account so you keep your progress on a new phone. Settings → Reset progress erases your levels, flowers, moves and Daily Rain dates. It keeps your settings, your chosen cloud and the levels you made; delete those one by one in the Workshop, or uninstall the app to erase everything.

Permissions

Internet, network state and billing: added by Google Play Billing, used only for the Supporter Pack as described above. Vibration: used for rain, lightning and the breathing guide. You can turn it off in Settings. Joy Dance reads the phone's motion sensor while that screen is open, only to detect dance moves. These readings are never saved or sent.

Children

Rain of Hope is suitable for everyone, including children. We collect no personal information from anyone. Players who answer that they are under 13 are never offered the Supporter Pack, and Google's billing library does not run for them.

Sharing

If you tap Share after a level, on a Daily Rain wisdom card, or on a level you made, Android opens its share menu with a short message (for a level you made, it includes the level's code). You choose where it goes; the app sends nothing by itself.

Sharing a level code from the Level Editor also copies the code to your clipboard so you can paste it. The app never reads your clipboard: to import a level, you paste the code yourself.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.

Contact

Questions or requests: email k7maligai@gmail.com, or open an issue at github.com/k2gsalem/rain-of-hope/issues.
